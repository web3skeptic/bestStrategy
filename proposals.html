Design proposals: Temples & the mid map

Three follow-ups to the two rule fixes shipped today (founder pinned to the site · standing Temples are solid). Nothing here is implemented — it's a preview to accept, tweak or reject. ← back to the game

1. Only fighters can break a founding founding interrupt

Today any living enemy unit in the 6-hex ring at resolution aborts the founding: a Healer, a Builder, a Range Booster, a 1 HP straggler. On the mid map someone is almost always adjacent, so centre Temples rarely complete, and 6 aura is lost with only a generic "founding failed" toast.

Proposal: only units that already project ZOC (Warrior/Pike/Cavalry/Heavy classes) count as an adjacent interrupt. Support and civil units can stand next to a founding site without stopping it. An enemy on the site still always blocks (unchanged). Show the 6-hex "contested" ring when a founding starts so the player can see what threatens it.

Proposed — completes

Healer ignored; a Warrior in the ring would still abort it.

2. Smouldering Ruins destroy → reconsecrate loop

Today a Temple knocked to Ruins can be reconsecrated the same round by any Temple unit already standing next to it, and the previous owner restores it at its old level (even Temple II) for the flat 6 aura, forever. Mid-map fights become a cheap ping-pong: the attacker spends 2–3 rounds of damage, the defender re-founds it in one action.

Proposal: fresh Ruins smoulder for 1 full round: nobody can start founding on them until the start of the round after destruction. The previous owner's level-restore memory expires after 3 rounds; after that, everyone rebuilds at Temple I. The attacker gets a real window to bring a founder up; the defender gets a real reason to hold the site rather than let it fall and re-buy it.

Now

Destroyed this round → defender's adjacent unit steps on and re-founds the same round.

Proposed

Ruins are locked one round; attacker's founder gets a move to reach it.

3. Temples muster on the ring, not the roof deployment + garrison

Since today, a Temple tile is solid. But the training queue still deploys new units onto the Temple tile itself; the unit can walk off but never back on. It also means one idle unit on the roof blocks every further deployment, and the owner has no way to "hold" a Temple physically.

Proposal: a completed unit deploys onto a free hex in the Temple's 6-ring (nearest to the owner's Capital first); the queue only waits if all six are taken. In exchange, the owner gets a way to defend it: each friendly fighter adjacent to a Temple gives it +1 DEF (max +2). A guarded mid-map Temple takes noticeably longer to grind down; an abandoned one falls as fast as today.

Now

Recruit spawns on the roof. Roof occupied → queue stalls. No garrison effect.

Proposed

Spawns on the ring toward home. Two adjacent fighters → Temple DEF 5 → 7.
