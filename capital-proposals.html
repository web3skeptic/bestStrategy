Capital cities — one look per tribe

Today both tribes share one ziggurat. Each row shows the current Capital on the left, then three faction-specific proposals, each with one of that tribe's units beside it for scale.

What each version is

Nomads — A · Great Yurt Camp: a grand team-felt yurt ringed by smaller yurts, a stake palisade, horse-skull totems, a campfire and a tall standard. B · Khan's Pavilion: one tall striped war tent with pennants on the mast, hitched horses, a spear rack and war drums inside a rope fence. C · Horde Hold: a raised earth mound — palisade and walkway on the lower terrace, a watchtower, the chieftain's yurt and totems on top.

Farmers — A · Granary Town: a timber-framed great hall with a team-tiled roof, granary silo, windmill, wheat rows, haystacks and a well inside a low stone wall. B · Windmill Keep: a big stone windmill with a team-coloured cap as the centrepiece, a barn, twin silos, an orchard and a hay cart. C · Walled Farmstead: a two-storey farmhouse with a smoking chimney and a barn behind a proper stone wall with a gatehouse, plus an animal pen, oak tree and scarecrow.
